◆

Credential protection

  • Credentials are encrypted using the operating system's secure storage.
  • The desktop renderer never receives your Application Password.
  • All website requests require HTTPS.
  • The WordPress connector checks the signed-in user's capabilities on every action.
  • No public cloud relay is used by this app.
Anyone who can unlock your Windows account may be able to use its saved connection. Lock your PC when away and disconnect on shared computers.
